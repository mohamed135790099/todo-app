# Todo App

Welcome to Todo App! Todo App is a simple and intuitive application designed to help you organize your tasks and manage your to-do lists effectively. With Todo App, you can stay productive and focused on your goals by keeping track of your tasks and prioritizing your work.
![todo_app](https://github.com/mohamed135790099/todo-app/assets/69519057/3103f5f6-b31f-45b6-b30e-ca8f13c79d95)

## Features:

1. **Task Management:**
   - Easily create, edit, and delete tasks to keep your to-do list up-to-date.
   - Organize tasks into categories or projects for better organization and clarity.

2. **Priority Levels:**
   - Assign priority levels to tasks to indicate their importance and urgency.
   - Focus on high-priority tasks and stay on top of deadlines.

3. **Due Dates and Reminders:**
   - Set due dates and deadlines for tasks to ensure timely completion.
   - Receive reminders and notifications to stay on track and avoid missing deadlines.

4. **Task Notes and Descriptions:**
   - Add detailed notes and descriptions to tasks to provide context and additional information.
   - Keep all relevant information about a task in one place for easy reference.

5. **Task Status Tracking:**
   - Track the status of tasks, including completed, in progress, and pending tasks.
   - Monitor your progress and celebrate your achievements as you complete tasks.

6. **Cross-Platform Sync:**
   - Sync your tasks across multiple devices and platforms to access them anytime, anywhere.
   - Stay productive on the go with seamless synchronization between your devices.

## Getting Started:

To get started with Todo App, follow these steps:
1. Download the Todo App from the App Store or Google Play Store, or access it via the web browser.
2. Sign up for an account or log in using your existing credentials.
3. Start creating your first to-do list by adding tasks and setting priorities and due dates.
4. Stay organized and focused by managing your tasks efficiently with Todo App.
